Paslago Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 November 2026

In short

Paslago sends files and text directly between devices. The developer, Ahmet OZCAN, does not collect, store or see your files, text or personal information. There are no accounts, ads or usage analytics. Paslago has no server.

What is processed on your device

The files you choose and the text you type are sent only to the devices you select, over an encrypted connection.

Your device stores: your app settings, your device name, the key and certificate that identify your device, the name, type and certificate fingerprint of devices you have exchanged something with, the devices you trust, and the files you receive. Received text is not saved as a file.

What devices on your network can see

While Paslago is open, devices on the same network can see your device’s name, type, local network address, port number and certificate fingerprint. You can change the device name in Settings. Recipients see the name, size and content of what you send.

When you use a direct connection and a QR code, the same information, together with the name and password of the temporary Wi-Fi network if there is one, passes to the device that scans the code.

Permissions

Local network and nearby devices: to find devices, connect and open a temporary network.

Camera: only to scan a QR code. Images are not saved or sent.

Photos and files: only for what you choose to send.

Notifications: to show incoming requests and transfer results.

Location: asked only on Android 12 and earlier, because the system requires it to open a temporary network. Your location is not read or stored.

Third parties

The Android version uses Google ML Kit to scan QR codes. Images are processed on your device. ML Kit may send diagnostic data such as device information, performance measurements, error codes and a scan session identifier to Google in encrypted form. Details: https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure

The store you download the app from may collect download and crash data under its own privacy policy.

Keeping and deleting

Data stays on your device. You can remove trusted devices in Settings and delete received files. Uninstalling the app usually deletes its settings; files saved to a downloads folder and settings files on computers may remain.

Your rights

Because no data reaches the developer, the developer holds nothing of yours to access, correct or delete. You can write to the address below with questions.

Children

Paslago does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page. Contact: 4ahmetozcan@gmail.com
